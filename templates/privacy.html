Privacy Policy

Aurora Play (iPhone) and Aurora (Apple TV). Last updated: November 2026.

Aurora Play is a party-game app: the TV is the board and phones are the controllers. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This page says exactly what the app and its server handle.

What the app handles

  • Your display name. What you type, or your Game Center name if you have not typed one. Other players in your room and the TV see it.
  • A random device ID. The app makes it on first launch. It is not your advertising ID and cannot be linked to you outside this app. It lets the app remember your profile and rejoin your seat after a dropped connection.
  • Game Center. If you are signed in, the app sends a short one-way hash of your Game Center player ID so a TV can recognise you between games. Apple's own ID is never sent to us.
  • Your results. Games played, wins, scores, daily challenge scores, and the device IDs of people you played with (for friends boards).
  • Things you type to create content. A quiz topic or a party name, for example.

Third parties that receive text

  • AI question writers (Google Gemini, OpenAI) receive a topic you type, so they can write questions. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • Text-to-speech (OpenAI, ElevenLabs) receives the lines the TV host speaks, which can include a name you entered for a party, such as a birthday name. The Apple TV can also speak offline with built-in voices, and then nothing is sent.

These providers handle that text under their own policies. We do not send them your device ID or any account details.

Microphone and speech

Travel Mode can listen for spoken answers. Speech is turned into text on your device, and the app never stores or uploads the audio. You can say no to the permission and still play with taps.

Local network

The app asks to use your local network so a phone can find a TV or a laptop server on the same Wi-Fi. It does not scan or read other devices.

How long we keep it

  • A room lives in the server's memory while people are in it and is dropped after it goes quiet.
  • Profiles and scores are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • The Apple TV's house leaderboard stays on that Apple TV only.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, show ads, track you across other apps or sites, or build advertising profiles.

Children

Aurora Play is a general family game and is not aimed at children under 13. It asks for no personal details beyond a display name, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
