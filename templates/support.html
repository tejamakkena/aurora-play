How to play

  • Open Aurora on the Apple TV and pick a game, or start a Game Night.
  • The TV shows a room code and a QR code. On each phone, scan it, or open Aurora Play and type the code.
  • No accounts are needed. Signing in to Game Center puts you on the TV's house leaderboard.
  • Phone Play has games you can play on the phone alone, with no TV.

Common questions

It says it cannot reach the room, or takes a minute to start. The game server sleeps when nobody has used it for a while and takes up to a minute to wake. Wait, then try the code again. Phones and TV need an internet connection.

The TV voice sounds robotic. In the TV's Settings, switch the voice to Studio (online) for the natural voice, or Offline for the built-in one.

I am not on the leaderboard. Sign in to Game Center on the phone (Settings, Game Center), then play a game on the TV. Guests can play but are not ranked.

A question repeated. The TV remembers what a family has seen and brings in fresh questions every day. Tell us which game and we will look.

Delete my data. Email us with your display name and we will remove it.
